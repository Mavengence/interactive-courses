Team Workflows.

One person's breakthrough is everybody's baseline. Shared prompts. Shared CLAUDE.md. Shared gains.

⏱ 10 min◆ 3 interactives✦ +65 XP

The biggest team-wide Claude wins don't come from finding a better model. They come from circulating prompts that work. If one person figured out the perfect PR-review prompt, the other 40 on the team shouldn't have to rediscover it.

At Anthropic itself — where much of the codebase is now written with Claude Code — the pattern is the same: engineers focus on architecture, product thinking, and orchestrating multiple agents. The compounding advantage isn't the model, it's the shared scaffolding around it.

Three team artifacts worth maintaining

01 · prompt library

A repo or Gdoc with named, tested prompts for recurring tasks: PR review, standup summary, post-mortem draft, release notes.

02 · CLAUDE.md

Checked into the repo. Reviewed like code. Updated when conventions drift.

03 · eval set

A handful of realistic examples — input + expected flavor of output. Use it to check a prompt change didn't regress.

How to share a prompt well

A prompt that works on your laptop is not the same as a prompt your team can use. Before you send it:

  • Strip your specifics. Replace hardcoded project names with placeholders like <PROJECT>.
  • Add a one-line "when to use." Future-you will forget.
  • Show a sample output. Sets expectations in a way words can't.
  • Note failure modes. "Tends to over-explain; add 'no preamble'."
